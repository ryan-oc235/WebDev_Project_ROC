About John

Hi, I'm John O'Reilly. I've been in the car business for as long as I can remember, and I love a well-tuned engine. I'm a bit of a perfectionist: maybe even more with cars than with people, if I'm honest.

"If it rattles, it doesn't leave the lot. That rule also applies to customers who whistle."

Our story

  1. 1998 John buys his first car: a Ford Escort with three working doors.
  2. 2008 He opens Wheels on Fire with two cars and one very optimistic sign.
  3. 2016 We move to a bigger showroom. The sign stays.
  4. Today Over 2,000 happy drivers and a 12-month warranty on every car.
